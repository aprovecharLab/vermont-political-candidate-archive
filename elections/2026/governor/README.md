## Methodology Notes

The four analyses in this directory share a common rubric, measuring alignment with four social-democratic organizations: the Democratic Socialists of America (DSA), Social Democrats of America (SDA), the Socialist International (SI), and Social Democrats USA (SDUSA). Candidate analyses assess campaign platforms; the incumbent analyses assess revealed governing behavior (vetoes, let-passes, signatures, and executive actions). All compilations were fact-checked against the [Vermont Legislature's official trackers](https://legislature.vermont.gov/bill/vetoed/2026) in September 2026; residual uncertainties are documented in each document's addendum.

## Archive Conventions

- Documents named with bill-focused titles are factual compilations; documents with "Lens" in the title are interpretive analyses.
- Each document carries a dated addendum noting known data gaps and uncertainties.
- Links should be verified against current sources before external citation; campaign positions and legislative data may have evolved since preparation dates noted within each document.

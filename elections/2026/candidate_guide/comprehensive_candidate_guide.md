# Vermont 2026 Primary Elections: Comprehensive Candidate Guide

## Overview

This document provides a comprehensive breakdown of the 2026 Vermont Democratic primary elections covering **statewide offices**, **Washington County legislative races**, and **Montpelier City offices**. Primary election day is **Tuesday, August 11, 2026**. General election day is **Tuesday, November 3, 2026**.

### TOP 5 STRONGEST SOCIAL DEMOCRATIC ALIGNMENTS

1. **Andrew Perchlik** (Senate — Washington) — Strongest across all four organizations via Clean Energy Fund + Community Loan Fund
2. **Michael Pieciak** (State Treasurer) — Strongest SDA/SDUSA alignment via medical debt elimination, Vermont Saves, ESG investing
3. **Jared Duval** (House — Washington-4) — Strongest DSA alignment via union membership + climate advocacy career
4. **Charity Clark** (Attorney General) — Strongest SI alignment via environmental justice + 49 federal lawsuits
5. **Tim Ashe** (Auditor) — Strongest SDUSA alignment via AFL-CIO endorsement + institutional reform

### KEY PREDICTIVE FACTORS FOR SOCIAL DEMOCRATIC ALIGNMENT

1. **Labor union engagement** — Most predictive for DSA/SDA alignment (Duval, Ashe, McLaren, Perchlik)
2. **Environmental/climate work** — Drives SI/SDUSA alignment (Perchlik, Duval, Pieciak)
3. **Democratic expansion work** — Drives unique SI alignment (Copeland Hanzas voting task force, Waszazak voting age, Clark election defense)
4. **Office type shapes alignment ceiling** — Constitutional officers (AG, Treasurer, SoS) have more concrete records than legislators

## APPENDIX: ORGANIZATION PLATFORM REFERENCES

### Democratic Socialists of America (DSA)
Universal childcare, Medicare for All, free public college, Green New Deal, labor empowerment, living minimum wage, paid family leave, voting rights protection, anti-imperialist foreign policy, end money in politics

### Social Democrats of America (SDA)
Free education, universal healthcare, anti-death penalty, reproductive rights, universal basic income, right to unionize, separation of church and state, government makes life bearable for all

### Socialist International (SI)
Universal human rights, gender equality, climate justice, social solidarity, democratic economy under control, advanced participatory democracy, public ownership of productive resources, peace/anti-imperialism foreign policy

### Social Democrats USA (SDUSA)
Reformist pro-labor agenda, publicly funded healthcare/education, living-wage minimum, progressive taxation, expanded Social Security, affordable housing, strong collective bargaining, climate action (Green New Deal), reproductive rights, LGBTQ/civil rights, separation of church/state, multilateralist foreign policy
